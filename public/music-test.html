Music + Voice Test

A test page for Witness Reader. It checks one thing: does the reading voice stop your music on this iPhone?

How to test (about 3 minutes)

  1. Tap Pick a music file and choose an MP3 or M4A from Files.
  2. Tap Play music. Move the music slider to about 30%.
  3. Tap Cloud voice (the Polly voice the reader uses). Does the music keep playing under it?
  4. Tap Device voice (the iPhone's own voice). Same question.
  5. Lock the phone for 20 seconds. Does the music keep going?
  6. Tap Copy results and paste them to Claude.

If the music stops, try again with the other Music engine, then the other Audio mode.
